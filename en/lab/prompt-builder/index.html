LAB_01 / BUILDER

Build your music prompt decision by decision

Pick one option per axis. The block below assembles itself in the right order, ready to paste into the Styles field with Custom and Instrumental on.

Genre

Sets the rhythmic family and the tempo the tool assumes by default.

Drums

Decides where the hits come from and how much of the bar they fill.

Low end

Decides whether the low end carries melody or just holds the weight.

Lead source

Decides what the main motif on top of the beat is made of.

Space

Decides the size of the place the beat sits in.

Texture

Decides how much surface dirt the mix carries.

Feel

Decides how the rhythm breathes: exact, swung or dragging.
