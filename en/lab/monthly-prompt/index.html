The prompt of the month

One complete dark drill direction: a genre with no folder in the catalogue yet. Copy it, test it and look at how it is built.

UNDERGROUND DRILL · 2026-07
dark drill instrumental, cold sliding 808 with hard glides, dry punchy kick and snare, skipping ghost hats, detuned piano motif repeating without development, narrow concrete room, tense and minimal

How to use it

  1. Open Create and turn on Custom and Instrumental.
  2. Paste the complete block into Styles and generate two versions.
  3. Change one axis — the motif or the space — and generate two more to compare.

Look at the structure: genre, drums, low end, source, space and feel, one decision per slot. It is the same method behind the catalogue folders and the LAB builder.

If it drifts, exclude: EDM, drop, bright pop chords, orchestral strings, uplifting
